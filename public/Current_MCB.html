Back Pain

Back pain is one of the most common musculoskeletal complaints. We provide personalised treatment plans aimed at relieving discomfort and improving mobility.

Treated by
Licensed MD
40+ Years
Order of
Orange-
Nassau

Description & symptoms

Aching, stiffness or sharp pain anywhere along the spine, from a dull daily ache to pain that limits bending, sitting or standing for long periods.

The MC Balans approach

We assess posture, movement patterns and any underlying tension before building a plan — often combining acupuncture or acupotomy for targeted relief with nutrition and movement guidance to support longer-term mobility.
